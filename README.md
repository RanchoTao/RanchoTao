# Shengye (Rancho) Tao

Undergraduate in Mathematics & AI at [BIMSA](https://www.bimsa.cn/), Beijing.
I study how language models learn and reason, and I build systems that
automate parts of the research loop — while keeping a hard line between
*generated hypotheses* and *verified evidence*.

**Research interests:** interpretability and training dynamics of LLMs ·
automated research workflows · AI-assisted mathematics

### Research systems
- **[RanchoResearch](https://github.com/RanchoTao/RanchoResearch)** — research-automation engine with explicit evidence, review and reproducibility gates
- **[MathForge](https://github.com/RanchoTao/MathForge)** — framework for conjecture exploration with verification gates (Lean, computation, review)
- **[ConjMap](https://github.com/RanchoTao/ConjMap)** — interactive, evidence-traceable map of open problems in mathematics
- **[NOVA](https://github.com/RanchoTao/NOVA)** — generates testable challenges to default research assumptions

### Products
